Session 1 of 6 · Introduction
What is marketing strategy? 🚀
Marketing strategy is your game plan for reaching the right people, with the right message, at the right time — so they choose you over everyone else.
🎯
Right audience
Who are your customers?
💬
Right message
What do you tell them?
⏰
Right time
When do they need you?
📍
Right place
Where do you reach them?
💡 Real example: Nike doesn't just sell shoes — they sell the feeling of being an athlete. "Just Do It" isn't about products, it's about identity. That's strategy.
Session 2 of 6 · Research
Know your customer first 🔍
Before marketing anything, you must know exactly who you're talking to. This is called your target audience.
👤
Who are they?
Age, gender, job, location, lifestyle
❤️
What do they want?
Dreams, goals, desires, aspirations
😓
What's their pain?
Problems, frustrations, struggles they face daily
📱
Where do they hang out?
Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google, LinkedIn
💰
What can they afford?
Budget range, spending habits, price sensitivity
☕ Example: A coffee shop near a university targets students aged 18–25 who need energy to study and love Instagram-worthy aesthetic drinks under $6.
Session 3 of 6 · Framework
The 4 Ps of marketing 🏆
Every marketing strategy is built on 4 pillars. Master these and you can market anything.
P
Product
What are you selling?
e.g. Handmade organic soap bars
P
Price
How much do you charge?
e.g. $12 per bar (premium)
P
Place
Where do you sell it?
e.g. Farmers market + Etsy shop
P
Promotion
How do people find out?
e.g. Instagram reels + influencers
🍎 Apple's 4 Ps: Premium product design · High price (status signal) · Apple Stores + online (place) · Sleek emotional ads (promotion). All 4 work together perfectly.
Session 4 of 6 · Positioning
Your unique value proposition 💎
A UVP answers: "Why should I choose YOU over everyone else?" — It's the heart of your entire marketing.
"I help [who] achieve [what result] by [your way] — unlike [the alternative]"
🚗 Uber's UVP
"We help busy city people get a safe, affordable ride in minutes — unlike waiting for taxis with no idea when they'll arrive."
🥐 Local bakery UVP
"We help health-conscious families enjoy guilt-free pastries made with natural ingredients — unlike stores that use preservatives and artificial flavors."
💡 The clearer your UVP, the easier your entire marketing becomes.
Session 5 of 6 · Strategy
The customer journey 🗺️
Customers don't buy instantly. They pass through stages — and your marketing must guide them through each one.
1
Awareness — They discover you exist
Ad, social post, word of mouth, search result
Starbucks: "Saw a seasonal drink on Instagram"
2
Interest — They want to learn more
Visit your website, follow your page, watch your content
Starbucks: "Downloaded the app, browsed the menu"
3
Decision — They consider buying
Compare prices, read reviews, check alternatives
Starbucks: "Checked prices vs local café"
4
Action — They buy! 🎉
Purchase, sign up, book, subscribe
Starbucks: "Ordered the Pumpkin Spice Latte"
5
Loyalty — They come back AND tell friends ⭐
Repeat purchases, referrals, reviews, brand advocates
Starbucks: "Gets the rewards card, tells 3 friends"
